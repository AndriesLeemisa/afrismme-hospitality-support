### 1. Executive Brand Architecture & Strategy

#### 1.1 The Strategic Purpose
AfriSMME Hub is an integrated hospitality environment, operations, maintenance, compliance, and digital support partner. It exists to solve the critical commercial failure point of multi-vendor fragmentation: hotel and restaurant operators are routinely burdened with managing 8 to 12 disconnected contractors (shade providers, carpenters, upholsterers, cleaners, compliance auditors, and web developers) with zero centralized accountability.

AfriSMME Hub unifies these competencies into a single coordinated ecosystem governed under one commercial service agreement, one quality standard, and one direct executive contact.

#### 1.3 Core Operating Principles
1. **Scheduled & Phased Daytime Coordination**: Work is executed during scheduled daytime maintenance windows, quieter trading periods, and off-site workshop batches. Venues are returned sanitized, reset, and 100% ready for guest service.
2. **Substantiated Architectural Competence**: Communications must reflect calm authority, engineering rigor, and deep operational understanding. Cheap promotional buzzwords ("best", "cheapest", "revolutionary") are strictly prohibited.
3. **The 5-Step Continuous Improvement Cycle**:
   - `01 IDENTIFY` — Rigorous venue diagnostic and asset condition audit.
   - `02 RECOMMEND` — Prioritized, costed scope matrix with clear guest impact.
   - `03 IMPLEMENT` — Specialist craft execution under phased zonal & workshop schedules.
   - `04 MAINTAIN` — Scheduled preventative maintenance preventing asset decay.
   - `05 GROW` — Operational telemetry, digital menus, and yield optimization.

### 3. Typographic Hierarchy & Layout Rules

The typography is structured around a refined three-tier hierarchy that bridges editorial refinement, corporate readability, and engineering telemetry.

```
TIER 1: EDITORIAL SERIF       →  Cormorant Garamond (Headings, Pull Quotes, Title Spreads)
TIER 2: ARCHITECTURAL SANS    →  Plus Jakarta Sans (Body, Subheadings, Structural Copy)
TIER 3: TELEMETRY MONOSPACE   →  JetBrains Mono (Pillar Codes, Metrics, SLA Timestamps)
```

#### 3.1 Font Family Roles & Weights
- **Primary Serif**: `Cormorant Garamond` (Light 300, Regular 400, Medium 500, SemiBold 600, Italic).  
  *Characteristics*: High contrast strokes, classical elegance, graceful serifs. Used for page titles, chapter numerals, executive quotes, and book covers.
- **Primary Sans**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Regular 400, Medium 500, SemiBold 600, Bold 700).  
  *Characteristics*: Clean geometric grotesque with generous x-height. Highly legible across dense tables, long-form editorial paragraphs, and corporate body text.
- **Technical Monospace**: `JetBrains Mono` / `Space Mono` (Regular 400, Bold 700).  
  *Characteristics*: Engineered clarity, distinct coding glyphs. Used for step counters (`01`, `02`), technical specifications, page numbers, and timestamps.

#### 4.1 Page Geometry Specifications
- **Trim Size**: 210.0 mm (Width) × 297.0 mm (Height) — Portrait Orientation.
- **Bleed Allowance**: 3.0 mm on all exterior edges.
- **Top Margin**: 18.0 mm (allowing 8mm clearance for running header).
- **Bottom Margin**: 18.0 mm (allowing 8mm clearance for running footer and folio).
- **Outer Margin**: 15.0 mm.
- **Spine (Gutter) Margin**:
  - *Saddle-Stitched (Document B — 12 Pages)*: 15.0 mm.
  - *PUR Perfect-Bound (Document A — 22 Pages, Document C — 36 Pages)*: 20.0 mm to accommodate spine milling and glue creep.
- **Grid Structure**:
  - *12-Column Compound Grid*: Standard across Document A and Document C. Column width: 11.2 mm; Gutter: 4.0 mm.
  - *6-Column Dynamic Grid*: Standard across Document B. Column width: 26.5 mm; Gutter: 5.0 mm.

### 5. Architectural Vector Diagram Conventions

All process and ecosystem diagrams must be rendered using vector precision (SVG / EPS). The visual design of diagrams must match the architectural aesthetic of the publication:

#### 5.1 Diagram Styling Rules
- **Backgrounds**: Botanical Forest Green field (`#132A1F` or `#1B3B2B`) with subtle outer border (`#28523E`).
- **Connecting Lines**: 1.0 pt to 1.5 pt stroke width in Bronze (`#C5A880`) or Muted Sage (`#3C7257`). No rounded bouncy cartoon curves; use clean 90-degree orthogonal connectors or precise geometric circles.
- **Node Geometry**: Sharp or 2px micro-rounded rectangles with forest fill (`#1B3B2B`) and high-contrast bronze or ivory typography.
- **Visual Balance**: Maintain minimum 20px padding between vector bounding boxes and adjacent typography.

### 7. Editorial Voice & Writing Governance

#### 7.1 Tone of Voice
- **Calm Authority**: Present capabilities with quiet confidence. Let technical depth, preventative protocols, and operational workflows demonstrate expertise.
- **Hospitality Fluent**: Understand covers, turning tables, breakfast shifts, RevPAR, acoustic privacy, and patio weather exposure.
- **Commercial Acumen**: Frame physical maintenance as asset protection, yield maximization, and guest review preservation.

#### 7.2 Terminology Glossary & Prohibited Words
- **Prohibited Words**: "cheap", "amazing", "one-stop-shop", "unbeatable", "the best in South Africa", "handyman", "revolutionary", "miracle".
- **Approved Equivalents**: "cost-effective", "rigorous", "single coordinated ecosystem", "benchmark standard", "specialist craft network", "turnkey facility support", "systematic".
