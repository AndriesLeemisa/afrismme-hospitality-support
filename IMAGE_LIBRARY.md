### Executive Overview & Visual Curation Standards

This document establishes the official visual catalogue for all high-resolution imagery assets utilized across the **AfriSMME Hub Corporate Publication System**, including:
- **Document A**: Corporate Company Profile (22 Pages)
- **Document B**: Premium Sales Brochure (12 Pages)

- **Digital Platform**: Next.js Executive Portal & Interactive Viewers

#### Visual Quality Mandate
The publication imagery adheres strictly to high-end architectural editorial standards (*Architectural Digest*, *Condé Nast Traveler*, *Hospitality Design*, *Dezeen*). The imagery avoids generic stock photography, saturated synthetic tones, and unnatural AI artifacts. Every image visualizes tangible hospitality environments, structural assemblies, artisanal craftsmanship, or operational workflows.

#### Mandatory Editorial Captioning Policy
In accordance with professional corporate governance and transparency standards:
- Any conceptual or AI-rendered visual featured in executive or print collateral must carry the discrete editorial attribution:  
  `Representative visualisation` or `Illustrative operational concept`.
- Unsupported claims, fictitious venue names, and fake branding marks are strictly prohibited.

### Technical Reproduction & Pre-Press Standards

#### 1. Color Management & ICC Profiles
- **Print Master Profile**: ISO Coated v2 (ECI) / FOGRA39 CMYK.
- **Maximum Ink Coverage (TAC)**: 300% to prevent ink set-off on uncoated woodfree papers.
- **Rich Black Specification**: C=60%, M=50%, Y=40%, K=100% (Neutral Deep Charcoal tone).
- **Fine Typographic Black**: C=0%, M=0%, Y=0%, K=100% (Strictly overprinted to eliminate registration ghosting).
- **Web / Digital Profile**: sRGB / Display P3 with gamma 2.2.

#### 2. Resolution & Scaling Thresholds
- **Critical Minimum Resolution**: 300 DPI at 100% reproduction scale.
- **Double-Page Spread (A3 width)**: Minimum image width required = 4961 pixels (420mm + 6mm bleed).
- **Full Page (A4 width)**: Minimum image width required = 2480 pixels (210mm + 6mm bleed).
- **Interpolation Algorithm**: Bicubic sharper for downsampling; Lanczos3 for pre-press rasterization.

#### 3. Bleed and Safe Zone Tolerances
- **Trim Box**: Standard ISO A4 (210 mm × 297 mm).
- **Bleed Box**: +3 mm all edges (Total canvas: 216 mm × 303 mm).
- **Inner Safety Margin**: 15 mm from trim edge (20 mm from inner spine edge for PUR bound documents exceeding 32 pages).
